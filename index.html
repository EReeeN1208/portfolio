Eren Kural

Incoming Senior at Robert College, Co-Captain of FRC team ARC6014

I study at Robert College of Istanbul. I want to study Computer Engineering and Electrical and Electronics Engineering, and I'm keen on coding, building computers, and robotics.

I'm a FIRST Leadership Award Finalist and co-captain of my Robotics Team. I competed at the Houston FIRST Robotics Championship in 2024 and 2025 with my team, and we received the Impact Award twice, in part for two projects I led: The first is DragonScout, an app making FRC scouting easier for small-mid sized teams. It is available on the App Store. The other is Arcinator, the worlds first free, publicly accessible FRC chatbot.

I built my first computer when I was 9, and I've since built all the computers used in my parents' architectural office. More recently, I built a guitar amplifier and two CNC machines.

I'm also passionate about playing and composing music. Currently, I serve as the head guitarist of the Robert College Orchestra.
